Participants were selected from a longitudinal dataset on OpenNeuro.org (see data descriptor Wang et al., 2022) based on the following criteria: (1) right-handed as indicated by 3 out of 5 handedness questions (children needed to pretend they write, erase, pick, open, and throw something) at the first time point entering the project; (2) typical nonverbal IQ as indicated by no less than 70 in the standardized score of the nonverbal test in the Kaufman Brief Intelligence Test (KBIT-2, Kaufman & Kaufman, 2004); (3) typical language skills as indicated by no less than 70 in the standardized score of the core language scale in the Clinical Evaluation of Language Fundamentals (CELF-5, Wiig et al., 2013); (4) having completed both runs of the meaning judgement fMRI task and the Word Identification (ID) subtest in the Woodcock-Johnson III Test of Achievement (WJ-III, Woodcock et al., 2001); and (5) no excessive movement as well as acceptable in-scanner accuracy for both runs of the fMRI meaning task.
